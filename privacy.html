Privacy Policy

Your Strategy · Effective Date: November 20, 2026

This policy covers the yourstrategy.co website and Hiring Scalpel, Your Strategy's web app at hiringscalpel.yourstrategy.co. It explains what data each one collects, including data Hiring Scalpel receives from Google, and how that data is used, stored, shared and deleted.

Your Strategy's iPhone apps have their own policies, such as the Sole Account privacy policy.

This Website

yourstrategy.co is a static website. It has no sign-in, forms, cookies, analytics or advertising. GitHub Pages hosts it, and GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security, as its privacy statement describes. If you email us, we use your message only to reply.

Hiring Scalpel

Hiring Scalpel is an internal tool that turns job ads into paused Google Search campaigns in Your Strategy's Google Ads account, and reports how those campaigns perform. Only people Your Strategy invites can sign in.

Google User Data Hiring Scalpel Accesses

  • Sign in with Google (scopes openid and email): your Google account's email address, and whether Google has verified it.
  • Google Ads (scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/adwords), only after an administrator connects Google Ads in Hiring Scalpel's settings: permission to manage Your Strategy's Google Ads account. Google gives Hiring Scalpel a refresh token for that account.

Hiring Scalpel doesn't request your name, photo, contacts, email messages, files or any other Google data.

How Hiring Scalpel Uses That Data

  • Your email address: to check that you're on the list of allowed users, to keep you signed in, and to record in an activity log when you sign in and which jobs and settings you add, change or delete.
  • Google Ads access: to create paused Search campaigns (budget, location targets, keywords and ads) for job ads that Your Strategy's team submits, and to read campaign reports (impressions, clicks, cost, and the search terms that showed the ads) for its dashboard. Hiring Scalpel never turns campaigns on or raises budgets.

Hiring Scalpel uses Google user data only to provide these features. Your Strategy doesn't sell it, doesn't use it for advertising, doesn't use it to train AI models, and doesn't let people read it except to keep the app secure, to comply with the law, or with your permission.

How Hiring Scalpel Stores and Protects Data

  • Hiring Scalpel and its Postgres database run on Railway, Your Strategy's hosting provider. Secrets such as API keys live in the host's protected settings, not in the code.
  • Connections between your browser and the app, and between the app and Google, use HTTPS.
  • Only the app and Your Strategy's administrators can reach the database.
  • The sign-in cookie is signed, hidden from page scripts (HttpOnly) and expires after 7 days.

Sharing

Your Strategy doesn't share, sell or transfer Google user data. Hiring Scalpel relies on these service providers:

  • Railway hosts the app and its database.
  • Google Ads receives the campaigns the app creates.
  • Anthropic (the Claude API) receives the text of each job ad so it can write keywords and ad text. Hiring Scalpel never sends Google user data to Anthropic.

Your Strategy may disclose data when the law requires it.

Other Data Hiring Scalpel Keeps

  • Job ads the team submits (web addresses, page content and pasted text), the keywords and ads written for them, and the IDs of the campaigns created.
  • No data about job seekers. Ads send people straight to the employer's job page, and Hiring Scalpel never learns who clicked.
  • Two cookies: the sign-in cookie, and a 10-minute cookie that protects the Google sign-in step. No analytics or advertising cookies.

Keeping and Deleting Data

  • The Google Ads refresh token stays until an administrator clicks Disconnect in Hiring Scalpel's settings, which deletes it. You can also remove Hiring Scalpel's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Deleting a job deletes its stored content and keywords. Campaigns already created stay in Google Ads until someone removes them there.
  • Email addresses stay in the activity log while the app runs.
  • To have your data deleted, email ben@yourstrategy.co, and we'll delete it.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Hiring Scalpel's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Children's Privacy

Neither this website nor Hiring Scalpel is meant for children, and neither collects data from children under 13.
